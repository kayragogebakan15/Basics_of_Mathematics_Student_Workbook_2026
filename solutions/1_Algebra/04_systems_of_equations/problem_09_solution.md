# Exercise 9 — A System from a Sum Condition

## 1. Problem Statement
Find numbers $x,y$ satisfying
$$x+y=10,\qquad 2x+5y=32.$$
Solve the system and explain geometrically why the solution is unique.

## 2. Method and Key Formula
Use elimination to solve the two linear equations. Geometrically, each equation represents a line in the plane. A unique solution occurs when the two lines have different slopes and therefore intersect at exactly one point.

## 3. Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
The system is
$$\begin{cases}x+y=10,\\2x+5y=32.\end{cases}$$
Multiply the first equation by $-2$ and add it to the second:
$$(-2x-2y)+(2x+5y)=-20+32,$$
$$3y=12\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=4.$$
Substitute into $x+y=10$:
$$x+4=10\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=6.$$

## 4. Verification and Consistency Checks
Check both conditions: $6+4=10$ and $2(6)+5(4)=12+20=32$. The line equations can be written as $y=10-x$ and $y=\frac{32-2x}{5}=\frac{32}{5}-\frac25x$. Their slopes are $-1$ and $-\frac25$, which are different, so they intersect at exactly one point.

## 5. Final Answer
$$\boxed{(x,y)=(6,4).}$$
