# Exercise 4 — Parabola

## 1. Problem Statement
For $y^2=12x$, determine the vertex, axis of symmetry and opening direction. Find the points corresponding to $x=3$ and use them to sketch the curve.

## 2. Theoretical Background and Concepts
The standard form $y^2=4px$ has vertex $(0,0)$, axis $y=0$, and opens toward positive $x$ when $p>0$. Squaring $y$ makes the equation unchanged under replacing $y$ by $-y$, so points occur in pairs reflected across the $x$-axis.

## 3. Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
Compare $y^2=12x$ with $y^2=4px$:

$$
4p=12\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p=3>0.
$$

The vertex is $(0,0)$ and the axis of symmetry is the $x$-axis. Since $p$ is positive, it opens to the right. At $x=3$:

$$
y^2=12(3)=36\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=\pm6.
$$

Thus the requested points are $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$.

Qualitative sketch (not to scale):

~~~text
             y
             ^
       (3,6) *        /
             |      /
             |    /
vertex (0,0) *---/--------------> x
             |    \
             |      \
      (3,-6) *        \
~~~

The upper and lower arms bend rightward from the vertex; the labeled points have the same $x$-coordinate and opposite $y$-coordinates.

## 4. Verification and Consistency Checks
For $(3,6)$, $6^2=36=12(3)$; for $(3,-6)$, $(-6)^2=36=12(3)$. The equation also implies $x=y^2/12\ge0$, confirming that there are no points with negative $x$.

## 5. Final Answer
Vertex $(0,0)$; symmetry axis $y=0$; opening toward positive $x$; points $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$.
